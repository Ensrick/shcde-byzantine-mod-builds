Our changes to Cyril43's axe:
- rescaled to 0.78 m;
- the lanyard at the butt removed;
- the leather grip wrap pressed thinner and the handle rounded where the hand holds it;
- only its colour textures kept, at a lower resolution.

Our changes to Taylor's axe:
- rescaled to 1.38 m;
- its plank-like haft made round: 31 mm across where the two hands hold it, easing to 36 mm under the head;
- its leather straps pressed flat onto the haft;
- only its colour texture kept, at a lower resolution.

Our changes to the shield:
- rescaled to 0.81 m across;
- the hand-grip bar on its back removed;
- only its colour texture kept, at a lower resolution.

Our changes to the mace:
- rescaled to 0.79 m;
- simplified to about a tenth of its triangles;
- the handle rounded where the hand holds it;
- a metallic finish added over its own colour and surface-detail textures.

Our changes to the javelin:
- rescaled uniformly to 1.71 m;
- its one grey material replaced by wood on the shaft and iron on the head;
- a leather grip binding, 31 mm across, where the hands hold it.

Our changes to the recurve bow and its arrow:
- the bow rescaled to 1.20 m tip to tip, the arrow to 0.73 m;
- the bow's leather grip made round, 31 mm across, where the hand holds it;
- its bowstring replaced by our own, strung over the recurves;
- its materials replaced by flat colours taken from its own look;
- in the quiver, only the arrows' fletched ends.

Our changes to the Magpie sword:
- its grip made round, 31 mm across, from the pommel to the guard, and the ring round the grip removed;
- only its colour texture kept, at a lower resolution.

Our changes to mypPi's kite shield:
- rescaled to 1.17 m from point to top;
- its leather and wood (the face, the back, the arm pad and the straps) turned a neutral grey that the
  game tints in the team's colour, keeping their grain as light and shade;
- a steel sheen added to the rim, the boss and the rivets over their own colours;
- only its colour textures kept, at a lower resolution;
- slung on the unit's back, shown without its arm straps.

Our changes to sheffrator's helmet:
- only the helmet used, not the set's mace or shield;
- its sculpted horse-tail removed: our own team-coloured horse-hair plume hangs from its finial instead;
- rescaled to 1.05 times and fitted to the rider's head;
- its scale aventail lengthened at the back and flared toward its hem, so it clears the head and covers the
  nape;
- a steel colour made from its ambient-occlusion map (it has no colour map), with its normal map kept.

Our changes to iedalton's axe:
- rescaled to 0.80 m;
- its leather grip made round, 31 mm across, where the hand holds it;
- its textures replaced by flat colours taken from them, with a steel sheen on the head.

- The CGTrader licence does not ask for credit. We give it because we are glad to.
- The shield's page also offers the licence's No AI variant. Our use fits it as well: the model is only
  rendered, and never used to train AI.
- The javelin and the recurve bow are under the No AI variant. Our use fits it in the same way.
- The Magpie's Sketchfab Free Standard licence lets us use it in the game and in renders, and does not
  ask for credit.
- The model files themselves are not part of this repository or of the mod. Only our renders of them
  are.
